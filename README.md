## April 30, 2026

I took some more measurements this evening to determine if a 2x4 can be positioned inside the permimeter of the tub (see diagram) without conflict with the tub itself... it can, nearly all the way around the 3 open sides of the tub.  In fact, the preliminary plan is to use a "C" shaped 2x4 frame with members laid down (so the 4" face on the ground), and another frame with members turned on their side (so the 2" faces facing up/down), and positioned 12" or so above the ground.  

Those frames should provide enough support to form a "C" shaped "wall" around the 3 open sides of the tub, formed using 16" lengths (or longer) of composite decking boards.  

## Material Possibilities

Menards sells UltraDeck® Natural™ Reversible Composite Decking in 4" width and 8', 12' (Model Number: 1122774 Menards ® SKU: 1122774) or 16' lengths for $1.39 per foot ($0.115833 per inch) after rebate.  At 16" length an 8' board provides 6 panels, a 12' would provide 9.  

Each 16" long board then costs $1.86 after rebate.  

I'd estimate it will take 20 4" boards for the 80" side, and 8 each for the 32" sides, so a total of 36 (or comfortaby 38) boards.  That's a minimum of $66.96 or maximum of $70.68 for the composite decking portion of the project.  

38 boards cut to length would require 5 12' planks with a little leftover for other projects.  Cost =  $83.33 after rebate.  

Grip Fast® UltraDeck T-Clip® - 100 Count (Model Number: C043317 Menards ® SKU: 2308124) fasteners cost $26.69 after rebate.  

AC2® 2 x 4 x 10' #2 Prime Ground Contact Green Pressure Treated Lumber is $6.85 after rebate.  I would need 2 of these totaling $13.70 after rebate. 

Total project cost = $83.33 + $26.69 + $13.70 = $123.72 after rebate.
